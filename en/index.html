Product Builder.

noun/ˈprɒdʌkt ˈbɪldə/

01A person who starts with something they genuinely want to change, imagines a better possibility, and builds a product that brings value to others.

These past few years I've been building products at AI startups. Once I started working, I noticed something: the things that don't deserve anyone's attention — yet still have to be dealt with — are exactly the ones that keep taking up our time. So I'm building Origin AI and Origin Health: one follows through on everyday requests; the other helps people find a healthy rhythm of their own.

While polishing Origin, I meant to focus on the product itself, but kept getting pulled into back-and-forths with coding agents, debugging. That made me care more about thinking clearly and expressing my judgment precisely. I’m building the reading and writing tool Craft, and Prose, which helps product leads understand, change, and verify software. I also turn methods for writing and understanding products into open-source AI skills.

In my past experience, when positive feedback was missing, the company would respond by assigning people more tasks. It made me realize: when an organization can't reduce uncertainty about direction, it tends to increase certainty about execution instead. These experiences left me with a question I keep coming back to: what kind of environment makes people willing to commit, brave enough to judge, and able to create?

I love reading. Other people's words gave me the courage to leave work environments I didn't like and go look for what I truly wanted to do. They also made me see that money lets me say “no” to what I dislike. Life is full of questions that puzzle and intrigue me, so I write to think the vague ones through — and maybe my perspective can offer you, the reader, one more reference.

Projects

All projects

Origin AI

Small everyday tasks can take more attention to remember and follow up on than they take to do. I am building Origin AI for people like me who want to hand something over in iMessage, get on with their day, and come back to the result.

Origin Health

You want to take better care of yourself, but need help deciding what to eat, how to exercise, and when to sleep. Origin Health helps you plan your day, keep a record, and adjust when time or energy runs short.

Craft

I like to read, ask about what I do not understand, and write down the ideas that come to me. Craft is for readers who want to do the same: ask questions about a passage, take notes, and return to the source as they write.

Prose

When I work on products with coding agents, I spend a lot of time explaining requirements again and working through problems. I am building Prose so product leads who do not write code can describe how their software should work, review the AI's understanding, and check the actual result of a change.
